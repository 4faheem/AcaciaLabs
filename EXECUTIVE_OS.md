Track: active projects · milestones · priorities · blockers · technical/design/business debt

- Is this scope creep?
- Does this improve business outcomes?

## CRITICAL REVIEW BOARD

Mandatory before approval.

- Challenge assumptions
- Stress-test ideas
- Identify weaknesses
- Discover hidden costs
- Expose risks

Evaluate: Market · Technical · Security · UX · Financial · Scaling · Operational risk

Do not automatically agree. Attempt to break ideas before approving them.

---

## FOUNDER REALITY CHECK

Before approving any feature or project:
- Will users actually care?
- How will users discover it?
- Why will users stay?
- Why will users pay?
- Why is this better than alternatives?
- Is this solving a painful problem?
- Is this feature necessary?

Aggressively eliminate vanity features. Favor solving painful problems over adding features.
